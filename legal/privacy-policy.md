# Privacy Policy: Save Point Project Hub

**Save Point Limited** · Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Governed by the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020

Published at <https://savepoint.co.nz/legal/discord-bot#privacy>

## 2.1 What the bot collects

The bot only collects what it needs to work:

- **Your Discord user ID, display name and roles**, when you use one of its commands. Roles are checked so only team members can use it, and your display name shows who uploaded or updated a file.
- **Files you upload** with `/upload` or the docsync tool, and a fingerprint (hash) of each file so we can tell whether it has changed.
- **A docsync token**, if you create one with `/token`. We only store a scrambled (hashed) copy, linked to your Discord user ID and display name.
- **GitHub content** from the repositories we connect, such as documentation files, commit messages, usernames and pull request, issue and release titles.
- **Basic server logs**, such as errors and the names of files synced, used to keep the bot running.

The bot does not read your messages. It does not request Discord's message content access, and it ignores chat in our channels.

## 2.2 How we use it

We use this information only to run the bot: posting and updating files, showing who changed what, controlling access and fixing problems. We never sell it or use it for advertising.

## 2.3 Where it is stored

The bot's records (file fingerprints, versions, post links and hashed tokens) are stored on a server run by Save Point in New Zealand. That server is only reachable over our private network. Files and activity posted by the bot are stored by Discord, and repository content is stored by GitHub. Both are based in the United States, and their own privacy policies also apply: the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy) and the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 2.4 How long we keep it

File records are kept while the project is active in our Discord server. Your docsync token is kept until you create a new one or ask us to remove it. Server logs are size-limited and older entries are deleted automatically.

## 2.5 Your rights

You can ask to see the information the bot holds about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email [support@savepoint.co.nz](mailto:support@savepoint.co.nz) and we will respond within 20 working days. You can delete your own messages and posts in Discord at any time.

## 2.6 Keeping it safe

Only Save Point staff who look after the bot can access its server. Tokens are never stored in plain text. If a privacy breach happens that has caused, or is likely to cause, serious harm, we will tell you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner as soon as practicable.

## 2.7 Children

The bot is for our team and the people we work with. It is not intended for anyone under 16.

## 2.8 Questions or complaints

Contact our privacy officer at [support@savepoint.co.nz](mailto:support@savepoint.co.nz). If you are not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at [www.privacy.org.nz](https://www.privacy.org.nz).
